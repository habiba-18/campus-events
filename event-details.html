Gaming / Esports

Intel Campus Cup

13–14 October 2026 • 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM

Academic Building, Zewail City • Organized with Zewail Esports

About the event

Zewail City hosted the Intel Campus Cup together with Zewail Esports, bringing students together for two days of VALORANT matches, interactive challenges, multiplayer games, and prizes.

Students tested their skills, competed with their peers, and enjoyed a different kind of campus experience where gaming brought everyone together.

Agenda

Day 1 – 13 October

  • 10:00 – 10:30 Check-in and QR code scan
  • 10:30 – 11:00 Opening and welcome
  • 11:00 – 1:00 VALORANT group stage
  • 1:00 – 2:00 Break
  • 2:00 – 4:00 Multiplayer games

Day 2 – 14 October

  • 10:00 – 10:30 Check-in and QR code scan
  • 10:30 – 12:30 Interactive challenges
  • 12:30 – 1:30 Break
  • 1:30 – 3:15 VALORANT finals
  • 3:15 – 4:00 Prize ceremony and closing

Event media

Listen to a short welcome chime for the Intel Campus Cup.

Entry information

  • Price: Free
  • Entry: QR code sent by email after registration
  • Venue: Academic Building
  • Time: 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM

Ceremony

Graduation Ceremony 2026

5 November 2026 • 6:00 PM – 9:00 PM

Main Auditorium, Zewail City

About the ceremony

Join graduates, families, faculty members, and students to celebrate the Class of 2026. The evening recognizes the graduates' work and achievements and brings the campus community together for the next chapter.

Evening agenda

  • 6:00 – 6:30 PM Guest arrival and seating
  • 6:30 – 6:45 PM Welcome and opening remarks
  • 6:45 – 7:15 PM Address from university leadership
  • 7:15 – 8:30 PM Graduate recognition and certificate presentation
  • 8:30 – 9:00 PM Class photos and closing

What to expect

Please arrive early so you have time to check in and find your seat. Graduates should follow the instructions from their program for gown collection and lineup. Guests should keep their invitation ready at the entrance.

  • Formal dress is recommended.
  • Photography is welcome from the audience seating area.
  • Accessible seating is available; mention any access needs when registering.

Venue and entry

  • Date: 5 November 2026
  • Time: 6:00 PM – 9:00 PM
  • Venue: Main Auditorium, Zewail City
  • Entry: Free; registration is requested for graduates and guests
  • Bring: Your registration confirmation

Career Fair 2026

24 November 2026 • 10:00 AM – 3:00 PM

Student Activities Hall, Zewail City

About the fair

Career Fair 2026 connects students with employers, graduate programs, and professional communities. Explore opportunities in engineering, technology, research, and business; ask about internships and entry-level roles; and practice introducing your skills to recruiters.

Fair agenda

  • 10:00 – 10:30 AM Check-in and collect your fair guide
  • 10:30 AM – 12:00 PM Employer and organization booth visits
  • 12:00 – 12:30 PM Career pathways panel
  • 12:30 – 1:00 PM Break and networking
  • 1:00 – 2:30 PM CV feedback and internship conversations
  • 2:30 – 3:00 PM Final booth visits and closing

Make the most of your visit

Bring a few copies of your CV, prepare a short introduction, and note the organizations you want to meet. Ask about internship timelines, graduate roles, required skills, and how to follow up after the fair.

  • Business casual clothing is recommended.
  • Bring your student ID for check-in.
  • Tell the organizers about access needs in the registration form.

Venue and entry

  • Date: 24 November 2026
  • Time: 10:00 AM – 3:00 PM
  • Venue: Student Activities Hall, Zewail City
  • Entry: Free for students
  • Bring: Student ID and copies of your CV
